# Welcome to the Craig Documentation

Craig is the multi-track recording bot for Discord. Record your voice channels with separate audio tracks for each speaker - perfect for podcasts, interviews, and content creation.

## Get Started

Invite Craig to your Discord server and start recording in seconds. Just use `/join` to start a recording in your current voice channel.

**Required permissions:**
- Send Messages
- Connect 
- Change Nickname

<Aside type="tip">
For a complete guide on setting up Craig, visit [craig.chat/get-started](https://craig.chat/get-started).
</Aside>
