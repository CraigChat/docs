Cloud Backup is a premium feature that automatically uploads your finished recordings to your preferred cloud storage service.

<Aside type='tip' icon='star' title='Supporter Feature'>
Cloud Backup requires being a [supporter](https://craig.chat/supporter). Any supporter tier can link cloud storage.
</Aside>

## Supported Services

- Google Drive
- Microsoft OneDrive
  - Files will be under "Apps/Craig"
- Dropbox
  - Files will be under "Apps/CraigChat"
- Box

## Setup

<Steps>
1. Make sure you have an active [supporter subscription](https://craig.chat/supporter).

2. Log in to the [Craig Dashboard](https://my.craig.chat/).

3. Connect your cloud storage account (Google, Microsoft, Dropbox, or Box).

4. That's it! Your recordings will now automatically upload when they finish.
</Steps>

<Image src={uploadService} alt="Upload service selection" style="border-radius: 0.75rem; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;" />


## How It Works

When your recording ends, Craig will start processing your recording in the selected format and DM you once the upload has finished with a link to the file.

The filename will include the recording ID and date for easy organization.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where do my recordings go?

Your recordings are automatically placed in a folder named **"Craig"** in your cloud storage (unless specified above). You don't need to create this folder, Craig will create it automatically.

### Which account receives the upload?

The recording is uploaded to the account of the **person who started the recording**. If it was an auto-recording, it uploads to the account of the person who enabled auto-recording for that channel.

### Can I share benefits with my server?

<Aside type="caution">
Recordings will **not** be uploaded if the person who started the recording doesn't have cloud backup linked, regardless of server blessings. Make sure the person starting recordings has access to Cloud Backup.
</Aside>

### How long until my recording is uploaded?

Upload time depends on the length of your recording. You'll receive a DM notification when the upload is complete or if it has failed.
