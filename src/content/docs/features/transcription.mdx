Transcription is a premium feature that transcribes your recordings using [Faster Whisper](https://github.com/SYSTRAN/faster-whisper). We use [Runpod](https://runpod.io/?ref=4ery0ian) to handle our transcription processing.

<Aside type='tip' icon='star' title='Supporter Feature'>
Transcription requires being a **Tier 3 Supporter**. [Become a supporter](https://craig.chat/supporter) to unlock this feature.
</Aside>

If you are a Tier 3 Supporter, you can go into any non-expired recording and check the download page for a new Transcription section.

<Image src={transcriptionSection} alt="Transcription section" style="border-radius: 0.75rem; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;" />

You can have your transcription in WebVTT, SubRip text (SRT), or in plain text. WebVTT also includes per-word timestamps, which works well for captioning.

<Image src={transcriptionModal} alt="Transcription modal" style="border-radius: 0.75rem; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;" />

For recordings with interjecting speech, Craig will try to separate speech into useable caption-like sentences.
